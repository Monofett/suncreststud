First White Galloway Calf in Chile – April 2025

The export of Suncrest White Galloway genetics (semen & embryos) to Chilean farmer – Ignacio Cifuentes, has to date resulted in the first pure-bred bull calf to be born in Chile, forming the start of the country’s first foundation White Galloway herd.

Ignacio farms a herd of Angus cattle on the island of Chiloe, a close offshore island 1250km south of Santiago at a latitude of ~43 degrees South, similar latitude to New Zealand’s mid South Island. The many attributes of the heritage Galloway cattle appealed due to the similarities in environmental and climatic conditions on Chiloe Island to those of Galloway’s homeland – Scotland, and New Zealand, and so Ignacio decided to proceed with establishing the first herd of White Galloway, Riggitts and Standard (Black, Red & Dun) Galloways in Chile.

Genetics for the various Galloway colour patterns were sourced from studs in both New Zealand and Canada, and Angus cows on farm, in Chile, were used as surrogate mothers.

The first White Galloway bull calf was born in September 2024 and fortunately was a very well marked (white with strong black points) with very good conformation, which should prove to be an excellent stud sire. Temperament was also favourable, the calf exhibiting a very laid-back attitude, a most important trait for breeding stock. The calf was named “el Maravilla” – which Ignacio explained appropriately, means “The Wonder” in Spanish.

Suncrest el Maravilla is doing well, weighing in at 300kg at 7 months old on 100% grass and mother’s milk, and has gained 1.35kg per day through late summer/autumn.

Fortuitously, Ignacio was able to attend the World Galloway Congress. hosted by the Galloway Cattle Society of New Zealand, in Queenstown, in April 2025, and this gave him a great introduction to the breed and a wonderful opportunity to meet in person with breeders from 13 countries, and particularly those breeders who provided the foundation genetics for his herd.

We look forward to receiving updates on the arrival of future progeny and wish Ignacio all the very best with his breeding.

World Galloway Congress Over the Years – a New Zealand Perspective - March 2025

The Galloway Cattle Society of New Zealand Inc. (GCSNZ) has been involved in World
Galloway Congress (WGC) since their inception in Alsfed, Germany in 1998, with past
president Barry McAlley being one of the foundation members, having attended a total of 12
bi-annual Congresses.

6th WGC – Auckland, New Zealand, 2008

In 2008 the GCSNZ had the privilege of hosting the 6th 2008 World Galloway Congress for the
first time in New Zealand in Auckland. Delegates from 8 countries attended the 2-day
Congress which was followed by a 3-day farm tour of selected North Island studs. Bob &
Karen Curry attended the Congress Proceedings along with other GCSNZ members, and then
accompanied the delegates on their North Island farm tour through the Waikato, Bay of
Plenty and on down to Wellington where they had the honour of hosting the visitors at
Suncrest on the final leg of the tour.

This was Bob & Karen’s introduction to WGC, the farm tours and social gatherings attended,
enabling them to meet numerous delegates from a number of countries forging friendships
and associations which were to be strengthened over the coming years. Many ideas and
techniques were traded with the delegates and very useful support was received on their
herd management and breeding programme. Such were the benefits of the interactions that
they decided to join Barry on subsequent Congresses, starting with the 8th in Denmark in 2010.

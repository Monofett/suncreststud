Suncrest Smokey the Bandit

  • Registration: AMHA - A140597 / NZMHA - 1128
  • Date of birth: 10 November 1992
  • Height: 32.25”
  • Colour: Silver dapple bay pinto

Passing on his colour, conformation, presence and personality plus, Smokey as he is affectionately known, has a wonderful temperament and is a pre-potent sire of numerous classy progeny (refer NZMHA Stud Book). During his exhibiting and show days, Smokey was always a perfect gentleman and was a pleasure to takeout. This attribute has been passed down to many of his progeny. Likewise he has shown these attributes when performing stud duties.
